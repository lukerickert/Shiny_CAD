# 15. Limitations

- Input is STEP only.
- Dimensions and notes are not written to the FCStd file.
- Dimensions in the DXF are lines and text, not DIMENSION entities.
- One section view per drawing.
- Sketch: arcs from three points cannot be drawn. Dimensions on a curve (radius of an ellipse, length of a spline) are not available; dimensions between curve points are. Imported splines and ellipses are polylines (**Convert to arc** / **spline** / **conic** in **Properties**, or **Convert N polyline(s)** in **Check**).
- Sketch: constraints are not written to the DXF file. The sketch file (**Save**) keeps them.
- Sketch: hatching is not imported or drawn.
- Sketch images: not exported to DXF or PDF. The lens model has one radial coefficient k1 with the distortion centre at the image centre.
- Part: fillet and chamfer are equal-distance on edges only; no draft. Chamfers of edges whose faces meet at less than 5.74 deg are not possible; fillets of such edges have an elliptic section (10.4, **Fillet**). Loft is a surface feature (**Loft surface**).
- Assembly: mates use planar and cylindrical faces, straight and circular edges, vertices and the reference planes and axes of the components. Gear mates measure rotations within half a turn of the reference position.
- Cosmetic threads and hole callouts come from the **Hole** feature of parts; threads of imported STEP files and of assembly drawings are not drawn. Holes copied by a **Pattern** or a **Mirror** carry the thread and the callout of the source hole, and the callout count includes the copies.
- FEA mesh: structured hexahedra need block cells (6 faces with 4 edges each); no swept meshing of non-block cells, no contact or boundary condition definitions.
- Analysis: static analyses with contact, plastic and hyperelastic materials and large deformation are solved as one nonlinear step with linear load ramps; beams and fasteners (U1 elements) only in linear solves; the Search of contact pairs compares the display triangles of the faces (no mesh). Shells: S4, S8R and S3; CalculiX expands a shell into one 3D element through the thickness (2 integration points through the thickness); more integration points through the thickness only with **Layers** on S8R (the CalculiX S4R element is not offered: its expanded C3D8R element is too flexible in bending on coarse meshes); members are not tied to shells; shell nodes are tied to solid faces by their translations; the reactions of edge and shell supports hold the forces and the moments of the forces about the support centre (no reaction moments of held rotations); **Position** of a shell refers to the element normal. Structured shell meshes: rectangles with edges along x, y or z only. Reference point couplings, joints and equations are linear (small rotations). Explicit: the /RBE2, /RBE3, /PROP/TYPE13, /MPC and the history output cards (/TH/NODE, /TH/PART, /TH/INTER, /SECT, /TH/SECTIO) are not checked against the OpenCourant Starter here; the `th_to_csv` column names of the history output groups are not verified. Modal and buckling analyses: solid bodies, plates and rods only. U1 beams: no shear deformation; torsional stiffness G (I11 + I22), exact for ROUND and PIPE only. Body plates are prismatic parts and plate items are flat; both have one element layer.
- Explicit: the ShinyCAD geometry gives first-order solid and shell elements (S4R, S4, S3); inp2rad does not read the shell offset. The deck generator models a flat rectangular panel with beams along x and y, one material and no rigid bodies (impactors come from an external .inp; the rigid floor applies). Supports hold the components of face and edge nodes (no prescribed displacements); loads other than the driver, gravity and the rigid floor come from the .inp. Initial state: the cards follow the OpenCourant hm_cfg_files (radioss2021) and are not checked against the OpenCourant Starter here; shells are written for N = 5 points through the thickness with the positions T = -1, -0.5, 0, 0.5, 1 and one surface point (the Radioss documentation lists Ishell 1 to 4 for /INISHE/STRS_F/GLOB; inp2rad writes Ishell 24); solid elements other than 8-node bricks and 4-node tetrahedra and shells other than S4R, S4 and S3 get no initial stress; the Explicit model has no fasteners, so the forces of the fasteners of the static solve are not transferred; the static solve uses the first-order elements of the Explicit mesh. Masses of the Explicit model have no rotational inertia; a point mass on a reference point away from a rigid body does not move the REF NODE of a deformable coupling. The results view reads the A-files of format FASTMAGI10 (OpenCourant) without the SPH particles, or legacy VTK files of anim_to_vtk. Tools: console tools get their own console window on Windows only; elsewhere their output goes to the console without input (prompts of inp2rad and of the rivet scripts are not answered). Job audit writes `job_audit.txt` and `job_audit.csv` into `drawgen/explicit/` unless `-o` is given.
- CAM: 3-axis milling (an Axis map writes A for a part on a rotary axis); the operations and dressups of [section 16](16-cam.md#16-cam); Pocket Shape on horizontal floor faces (sloping or curved floors go to 3D Pocket); Undercut on straight dovetails open at both ends; the 3D operations on a height map (no undercuts, one grid cell of margin on the walls at the sample interval); 3D Surface and Vcarve cut the solid stock by a triangulated height map surface of at most 100 points per side; thread milling cuts the stock by a ring (no thread form); the stock simulation of a shaped tool (ball, V bit, drill) uses stepped slabs of the profile; the playback moves the tool without cutting the stock step by step; placement constraints on planar and cylindrical faces, straight and circular edges and vertices; five post-processors (Estlcam controller, generic, GRBL, LinuxCNC, Mach3); the feeds and speeds table holds generic starting values; text engraving with the fonts installed on the PC (arial.ttf, DejaVuSans.ttf); sketches for Engrave and Vcarve on a plane horizontal in the job.
- Projection time depends on the number of B-spline faces (approximately 10 s to 30 s per part for a 7 MB STEP part with 234 faces on a 2-core machine). Views are computed in parallel on machines with more than 2 cores.
